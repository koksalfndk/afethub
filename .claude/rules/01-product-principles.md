# Product Principles

## Emergency First

AfetHUB may be used by people who are:

- Under stress
- Using a phone outdoors
- On a weak network
- Working with one hand
- Unfamiliar with technology
- Older or visually impaired

Interfaces must be simple enough to understand without training.

## Public Access

Public information should be accessible without authentication unless it contains private or operationally sensitive data.

Public users may view:

- Active disasters
- Published needs
- Remaining quantities
- Approved delivery quantities
- Public delivery locations
- Public announcements
- Public update times

Never expose publicly:

- Contributor email addresses
- Contributor telephone numbers
- Private coordinator notes
- Internal verification evidence
- Exact private residential locations
- Authentication metadata
- Abuse and risk scores
- Internal audit details

## Registration Must Be Optional

Do not place registration gates before public contribution forms.

After a successful guest submission, the user may be offered an optional account.

Account creation must reuse information already supplied by the user.

Never ask the user to re-enter:

- Full name
- Email
- City

when those values already exist in the submission flow.

## Clear Operational States

Every important entity must have an explicit status.

Avoid ambiguous states such as:

- Active
- Done
- Processing

without domain context.

Use clear states such as:

- Email verification pending
- Coordinator review pending
- Approved
- Partially approved
- Rejected
- Information requested
- Completed
- Paused
- Cancelled
- Archived

## No Misleading Urgency

Do not use emergency styling merely for attention.

Reserve red and critical labels for genuinely critical information.

Do not display fake countdowns, fake scarcity or unverified claims.

## Transparency

Public pages should clearly distinguish:

- Requested
- Approved as delivered
- Pending verification
- Remaining

Never combine pending and approved quantities into a single “received” number.

## Freshness

Every operational page must display a meaningful last-updated timestamp.

Stale data should be visibly marked.

Where practical, coordinators should be prompted to reconfirm old active needs.
